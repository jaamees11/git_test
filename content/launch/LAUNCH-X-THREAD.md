## Posting strategy

1. Post tweets 1–8 as a thread, NOT separate posts.
2. Pin tweet 1 to your profile.
3. **First 30 min is critical.** Reply to literally every comment, even 👍 — drives algorithmic reach.
4. **Don't edit** after posting (any edit kills reach on X).
5. **Quote-tweet** the pinned post weekly with a "oh btw here's last week's issue" follow-up to revive.

## Notes on voice

- **Lowercase first letter** on most tweets (feels conversational on X)
- **Bullets with specific numbers**, not generic benefits
- **Self-deprecating aside** in at least one tweet (the "sharp, not enthusiastic" unsub joke)
- **Never use** "game-changer," "revolutionary," "10x," "unlock." These kill credibility with the builder audience you want.
- **Do use** specific models, prices, numbers, names. Specificity = trust.

## What to do if the thread flops

First thread rarely takes off. If it gets <50 engagements in 24h:

1. Don't panic. Most threads fail.
2. Re-post variant 2 days later — different hook, same content.
3. Try hook variants: *"I got tired of scrolling AI Twitter, so I automated it"* or *"Here's a $0/mo AI newsletter stack I built in a weekend"*.
4. Reply to 3–5 big AI accounts' tweets with a thoughtful take that leads naturally to your pinned thread. Don't spam.
5. The thread that eventually takes off will look just like one of the failures. Keep shipping.
