## How to set up in Beehiiv

1. Beehiiv → **Settings → Paid Subscriptions**
2. Connect **Stripe** (takes 5 min; standard business account info)
3. Create two plans:
   - **Monthly:** $9.00 USD / month
   - **Annual:** $79.00 USD / year
4. Set **"Preview length"** for paid posts: 200–300 words (enough to tease
   without giving it away)
5. Enable **"Gifting"** if you want — friends/fans can gift paid subs
6. Grab the two **upgrade URLs** from that page
7. Swap them into `{{upgrade_monthly_url}}` and `{{upgrade_yearly_url}}` in
   the email above
8. Send via Beehiiv (as a normal post — don't use the automation flow for this)
