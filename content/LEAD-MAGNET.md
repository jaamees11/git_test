## Why this works

- **Specific number** ("50 tools") beats vague ("lots of tools")
- **Year in title** makes it feel current (update it yearly)
- **Job-to-be-done categories** (not "best AI tools") match how people
  actually search
- **Your 5-tool personal stack** at the end adds trust and keeps readers
  past the list skim
