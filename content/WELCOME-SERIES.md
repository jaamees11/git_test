# Welcome Series — paste into Beehiiv Automations

Beehiiv → **Automations** → **New Automation** → pick the trigger
**"Subscribed to [your publication]"** → add 3 emails below with the delays.

**Why 3 emails?** The first 72 hours after signup is when new subs are most
likely to reply, forward, and upgrade. One email gets lost; three builds a
habit and surfaces the paid tier without being pushy.

---

## Email 1 — send immediately (delay: 0 minutes)

**Subject:** You're in 👋 here's what to expect

**Body (paste into the Beehiiv post editor as regular paragraphs):**

> Hey —
>
> You're now subscribed to **AI Pulse**. Here's the deal:
>
> **🚀 Fresh Tools** — 3–5 AI launches from the last 24h worth clicking.

>
> **One ask:** reply to this email with the one role or problem you'd most like AI Pulse to cover. I read every reply and use them to shape what gets in and what doesn't.
>
> See you tomorrow morning.
>
> — AI Pulse

## How to set this up in Beehiiv

1. Beehiiv dashboard → **Automations** (left sidebar)
2. Click **Create Automation** → name it "Welcome Series"
3. **Trigger:** "Subscribed to [your publication]"
4. Add **Action: Send Email** → paste Email 1 above → set delay **0 minutes**
5. Add another **Action: Send Email** → paste Email 2 → set delay **2 days**
6. Add another **Action: Send Email** → paste Email 3 → set delay **7 days**
7. Click **Activate**

That's it. It runs forever, for every new sub, hands-free.
