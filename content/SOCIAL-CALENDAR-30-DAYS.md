# 30-Day Social Content Calendar

> Paste-ready X and LinkedIn posts for days 2–30 of the launch. Pairs 1:1
> with `30-DAY-PLAYBOOK.md`.
>
> **Why this exists:** the playbook tells you "post on LinkedIn today."
> Writing social posts from scratch when you're also trying to run the
> newsletter burns out most creators by week 2. This file removes that
> friction — every day has copy ready.
>
> Rules:
> - Swap `{{subscribe_url}}`, `{{archive_url}}`, and `{{sub_count}}` each time
> - Post times: X: 9-11 AM or 7-9 PM ET, Tue/Wed/Thu best. LinkedIn: Tue/Wed 7:30-9 AM local.
> - Skip a day only if you genuinely don't have content worth posting; don't post for the sake of posting.
> - Adapt tone to what actually happened that week. These are scaffolds, not scripts.

### Day 13 — LinkedIn reflection on "how to choose AI tools"

```
3 questions I ask before adding an AI tool to my workflow:

1. Does it replace something I already do, or does it create new work?
(Most "AI writer" tools create work. Most "AI summarizer" tools replace work. Different deals.)

2. Can I reproduce 80% of its value with Claude/ChatGPT + a good prompt?
(If yes, skip the $20/mo and add to your prompt library instead.)

3. Does it integrate with the tool I'm already in?
(Notion AI beats a better standalone tool 9 times out of 10 because it's where I already write.)

If a tool survives all 3, it earns a slot. Most don't.

I cover ~5 of these filters per week in AI Pulse: {{subscribe_url}}
```

## Anti-patterns (never post these)

- "Just hit 100 subs! Thanks everyone 🙏" — reads as begging
- Screenshots of generic quotes over sunset images — we're not motivational
- Lists that start with "Forgot X? Here's Y" that demand agreement — condescending
- "Agree? 👇" as a closer — desperate
- Anything that starts with "BREAKING:" unless it's actually breaking
