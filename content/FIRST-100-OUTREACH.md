# First 100 Subs — Personal Outreach Scripts

> The most awkward part of launching a newsletter is asking friends,
> family, and colleagues to subscribe. But it's also the single
> highest-conversion growth move you'll ever make. ~80% of friend DMs
> convert; no other channel comes close.
>
> This file removes the "what do I say?" friction. Copy → customize →
> send. Total time to hit 50 subs from your existing network: ~90 min.
>
> **Strategy:** don't mass-text. Personalize each one. The extra 30
> seconds per DM is what turns the ask from "spam from my contact" to
> "friend thought of me specifically."

---

## Before you send anything

Pick 30-50 people from each category below. DO NOT exceed 10 people per
day — you'll seem desperate and you won't have the bandwidth to reply
personally to who responds.

Day-by-day cadence (over 1 week):
- Day 1: 5 closest friends (the ones who'd definitely subscribe)
- Day 2: 10 colleagues / professional contacts
- Day 3: 10 "ambient friends" (people you see occasionally but like)
- Day 4: 10 industry acquaintances who care about AI
- Day 5: 10 family members in relevant professions
- Day 6: catch-up / rest
- Day 7: reply to everyone who replied, send 5 final asks

---

## Category 1 — Closest friends (highest success rate, ~90%)

### Text script (iMessage / WhatsApp / Signal)

```
hey — started a thing. tiny newsletter that collects the 5 AI things that actually matter each day, no hype, 2-min read.

would love you as a subscriber #1. no pressure: {{subscribe_url}}
```

**Variations if you want to be less direct:**

```
yo, working on an experiment. trying to see if i can build a tiny AI newsletter audience from scratch. if you have 30 seconds this week to subscribe it'd genuinely help me: {{subscribe_url}}

(it's actually useful btw — not junk)
```

```
[your name] update: launched a daily AI newsletter today. 2 min, no hype. just the actual 5 things that shipped.

subscribe if you want, don't if you don't: {{subscribe_url}}

also: what's new with you?
```

The last one is my favorite because it shifts the conversation back to them. Feels less transactional.

## Category 2 — Colleagues / professional contacts

### Slack DM to a current colleague

```
Hey {{name}} — started a side project: daily AI newsletter called AI Pulse. 2-min digest of the 5 things that actually shipped, no hype.

Would love you as a subscriber if AI stuff is useful to your work these days. No sweat if not:
{{subscribe_url}}

(If you do sub, reply back the first day with what you thought — I'm iterating.)
```

### Email to a past colleague you haven't talked to in 6+ months

```
Subject: (re-introducing myself + a small ask)

Hey {{name}},

Hope you've been well — I know it's been a minute. Quick update and a tiny ask.

Update: I've been heads-down on an AI side project. Launched a daily newsletter a few weeks ago called AI Pulse — a 2-minute read of what actually shipped in AI each day, no hype. {{sub_count}} subs so far.

Ask: if your job touches AI at all, I'd love you as a subscriber. It's free. And if it's useful, forward it to one person who'd dig it.

{{subscribe_url}}

No worries if not. Would love to catch up properly over coffee / call sometime.

— {{your_name}}
```

### LinkedIn message to professional contacts

```
Hey {{name}} — building a small thing: daily AI newsletter, 2 min, no hype. Would value your sub if you're curious: {{subscribe_url}}

Either way, good to see you on here.
```

*LinkedIn messages should be short. Anything longer than 2 sentences gets skimmed.*

## Category 3 — Ambient friends (people you see occasionally)

### Casual text / DM

```
hi! random: launching a newsletter today called AI Pulse. 5 things in AI that actually matter, daily, 2-min read.

would love you as an early subscriber — no pressure though: {{subscribe_url}}

hope you're good!
```

### If you see them in person (IRL ask — gold)

```
"Hey, random, but I started a little AI newsletter. 2 min a day, no hype. If you want to sign up I'd love it — just {{subscribe_url}} or I can text you the link."
```

IRL asks convert at ~95%. If you have a birthday party, coffee meeting, or any in-person event in the first 2 weeks — mention it.

## Category 4 — Industry acquaintances who care about AI

### Cold-ish DM (X or LinkedIn)

```
Hey {{name}} — I've been reading your {{specific_post / tweet / article}} on {{topic}} and wanted to say it was sharp.

I recently started AI Pulse — a daily 2-min AI digest with the same no-hype angle. ~{{sub_count}} subs so far.

Not asking for a shoutout or anything; just thought you might actually enjoy it as a reader: {{subscribe_url}}
```

### If they're a bigger AI creator (5k+ followers)

DON'T message them asking them to subscribe or promote. Do this instead:

1. Reply thoughtfully to 2-3 of their recent posts with a take or counter-example
2. After they've seen your reply a few times, send the DM above — but remove any "please subscribe" language
3. Accept that even if they do sub, they probably won't promote you. That's fine.

## Category 5 — Family

### Text to family members in relevant roles (tech, PM, marketing, operations)

```
hey — been working on a little side project. started an AI newsletter.

2 min a day, no hype. would love you as a subscriber, especially since {{role_specific_reason_it_could_help_them}}: {{subscribe_url}}

no pressure though. also: {{how_are_you_type_small_talk}}
```

### Text to family members NOT in relevant roles

Don't ask them to subscribe unless they'd genuinely use it. Your mum reading about Mistral model releases helps nobody. Instead:

```
hey — update: started an AI newsletter this week. going fine so far, ~{{sub_count}} people.

if you know anyone at work who works with tech or AI stuff, would you forward them this? thx! {{subscribe_url}}

hope you're well
```

This converts family into ambassadors without burdening them personally.

## Category 6 — Twitter/X followers you don't really know

### Generic DM (only do this with ~50 of your most-engaged followers)

```
hey — launching AI Pulse today. daily AI newsletter, 2 min, no hype.

if that's your thing, free sub: {{subscribe_url}}

if not, no worries. appreciate you being here either way.
```

**Warning:** X will flag your account if you DM 50+ people the same message in a day. Space it out (5-10/day), and customize at least the first sentence of each one.

## What to do when people reply

### "subscribed!"
```
thank you 🙌 means a lot. first issue hits {{when}}. would love your honest reaction after a week — good or bad.
```

### "sounds interesting, will check it out"
```
appreciate it. if you do sub: {{subscribe_url}} — happy to answer any questions about what it actually is.
```

### "not really my thing but good luck"
```
no worries at all, appreciate the honest response. cheers.
```

*Don't push. Their "no" is a future "yes" from a recommendation — only if you don't annoy them now.*

### "what's the angle? how is it different from [Rundown AI / TLDR / etc.]"
```
good question. main difference is voice — dry, no hype, no hedging. I don't cover every release; I cover the ones with actual numbers behind them.

also no sponsors in the content (ads live in a dedicated slot, not inside a blurb).

sample issue to judge for yourself: {{archive_url}}/issues/latest
```

### "can I repost / forward this to [my team]?"

YES. Say yes immediately. This is the single best signal you can get from an early sub.
```
absolutely, please do. if they want to chat feedback, I read every reply: {{your_email_or_twitter}}
```

## The compound effect

30-50 personalized DMs → realistically 20-35 subscribers. That's not a
lot. But:

- **They read every issue** for the first month (personal connection)
- **They forward it** at a much higher rate than strangers
- **They reply** with feedback that shapes the product
- **They become social proof** ("my friend [name] runs this…")

The first 50 subs from your network are worth more than the next 500 from
a cold launch. Don't skip this step because it feels awkward.

---

## What NOT to do

1. **Don't mass-text 30 people the same message.** Screenshots travel; someone will post it.
2. **Don't ask people to forward it in the first message.** That's a second ask, after they've replied positively.
3. **Don't guilt-trip non-responders.** They saw it; they passed. Move on.
4. **Don't ask for sponsorship / paid referrals from friends.** You're building an audience, not squeezing it.
5. **Don't send a follow-up "did you see my message?" 3 days later.** One ask. That's it.
