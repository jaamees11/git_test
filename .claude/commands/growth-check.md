Review AI Pulse growth progress against the 30-day playbook.

Steps:

1. Read `content/30-DAY-PLAYBOOK.md` to see what's supposed to happen each week.
2. Ask the user:
   - What day/week of the playbook they're on
   - Current sub count
   - Current open rate (approximate)
   - What they've shipped this week (issues, social posts, Reddit posts, DMs)
3. Compare to the playbook's targets for that week.
4. Produce a short report:
   - **On track:** items they've done that match the playbook
   - **Behind:** items they skipped or missed
   - **Ahead:** things they've done earlier than expected
   - **Recommended next action:** the 1-2 highest-leverage items for this coming week
5. Be honest. Don't pretend plateaus are fine if they've gone 10 days with no new subs — but don't panic if they're in a normal 3-5 day lull either.
6. Reference `content/COMPETITIVE-LANDSCAPE.md` benchmarks if comparing to industry norms.
